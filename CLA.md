> **DORMANT - NOT IN FORCE (2026-05-31).** SVCS v2 ships as an open-source
> project under **AGPL-3.0 only**; there is no commercial edition and no
> revenue split in effect. This CLA is retained as a *draft* in case the
> team later decides to fork a commercial edition (and only if the team is
> legally cleared to do so). It is not required to
> contribute to the open-source project, and no contributor is bound by it.
> Do not sign it. Revive it (with a lawyer) only if a commercial fork is
> actually pursued.

**Draft v1, 2026-05-14. Review with a lawyer before signing.**

This document sets out how contributions to SVCS are handled and how any
revenue from commercial licenses is shared among contributors.

## 1. Copyright

Each Contributor retains copyright in the contributions they author.
Nothing in this agreement transfers ownership of any contribution to any
other party.

## 2. Grant of license to the project

Each Contributor grants the SVCS project, and Kheiven D'Haiti acting on
the project's behalf, the perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free
right to:

a. Distribute the Contributor's contributions under the GNU Affero General
   Public License v3.0 (or any later version of AGPL).

b. Offer **commercial licenses** for the contributions, where a commercial
   license grants different terms than AGPL (typically removing the
   source-disclosure requirement in exchange for a fee).

c. Sub-license the contributions to commercial customers as part of a
   commercial license agreement.

## 3. Designated licensing administrator

**Kheiven D'Haiti** is designated as the licensing administrator. The
administrator is authorized to:

a. Sign commercial license agreements on behalf of the project for deals
   valued at **$25,000 USD per year or less**, without seeking individual
   consent from each Contributor.

b. Negotiate commercial license deals above the $25,000 threshold,
   subject to written consent from a majority of Contributors before
   signing.

The administrator role may be reassigned by unanimous written consent of
all Contributors.

## 4. Revenue sharing

Net revenue from commercial license agreements (gross revenue minus
direct costs such as legal fees, payment processing, and required
infrastructure) is distributed quarterly among Contributors per the
following schedule:

| Contributor | Share |
|---|---|
| Kheiven D'Haiti | 20% |
| Jorge Sanchez | 20% |
| Ashleyn Montano | 20% |
| Riley Roberts | 20% |
| Victor De Souza Teixeira | 20% |

(Equal split, 5 ways, as the starting allocation. To be revisited by
unanimous written consent if the team composition changes or if a
Contributor takes on substantially greater ongoing responsibilities.)

If a new Contributor joins after this CLA is signed, their share is set
to 0% until all existing Contributors agree in writing to a new schedule.

## 5. Distribution mechanics

a. Revenue is paid into an account designated by the licensing
   administrator (initially a personal account; to be replaced by a
   joint LLC or partnership account once total annual revenue exceeds
   $10,000 USD).

b. Within 30 days of the end of each calendar quarter, the administrator
   provides each Contributor with a statement showing: gross revenue,
   direct costs, net revenue, and each Contributor's calculated share.

c. Distributions are made by ACH, PayPal, or other mutually agreed method.

## 6. Departure and withdrawal

a. A Contributor may stop contributing at any time. They retain copyright
   in their prior contributions and continue to receive their share of
   revenue derived from those contributions.

b. If all Contributors agree by unanimous written consent, a departing
   Contributor's share may be reallocated among the remaining
   Contributors.

c. No Contributor may unilaterally revoke the licenses granted in section
   2 of this CLA.

## 7. New contributors

Anyone making a substantial contribution to SVCS (more than a one-line
fix) must sign this CLA before their contribution is accepted into the
main branch. Trivial contributions (typo fixes, documentation tweaks)
may be accepted under the project's open-source license without a CLA.

## 8. Disclaimers

a. Each Contributor warrants that their contributions are their own
   original work, and that they have the legal right to license them
   under this agreement.

b. Each Contributor's contribution is provided "as is" with no warranty
   of any kind.

c. The administrator acts in good faith on behalf of all Contributors.
   Disputes about revenue distribution or licensing decisions are
   resolved by majority vote of Contributors.

## 9. Amendments

This CLA may be amended by unanimous written consent of all current
Contributors.

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Florida, USA.

**Important**: this CLA is a starting template, not legal advice. Before
anyone signs, the team should run it past a lawyer. FAU's Small Business
Development Center (https://business.fau.edu/centers/sbdc/) offers free
consultations for students. Cooley GO (https://www.cooleygo.com) also
has free document templates.

Things a lawyer will probably want to tighten:

- Define "substantial contribution" more precisely
- Specify a dispute-resolution venue (arbitration vs. court)
- Add a clause about what happens if the project is sold or acquired
- Clarify tax treatment of revenue distributions (1099 vs. partnership K-1)
- Confirm whether forming an LLC changes the structure (it should, once
  there's real revenue)
