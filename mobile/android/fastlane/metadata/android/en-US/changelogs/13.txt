1.0.0-beta: SVCS now compresses video on the phone itself, no server needed.
- Quality presets, size limits (Discord, WhatsApp, Instagram, X, email, custom)
- H.265 or H.264, optional audio removal
- Smart Compress (beta): on-device detection squeezes footage where nothing happens
- Saved tab: searchable, filterable history of compressed videos
- Share in from any app, share results out
- Portrait videos keep their full resolution
